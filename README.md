# The Oval Market Brief

A standalone market brief for 15–20 The Oval, Bethnal Green / Cambridge Heath, London.

## Contents

- Commercial leasing: 20 comparables, with nearby candidates highlighted.
- Sale and investment: 15 historical records.
- Residential: 21 flat records, with three additional records listed separately.
- Three independent street maps and a concise asset-management recommendation.

## View

Download the repository and open `index.html` in a modern browser. No installation or build is required. Map images and tables are embedded; links to external sources require internet access.

## Data and mapping

Research snapshot: 16 October 2026. Transaction and listing fields are transcribed from supplied research materials; these are not live listings or an independent valuation. Commercial locations include approximate regional and postcode reference points. Residential locations use Postcodes.io postcode coordinates, not individual entrances.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
Postcode lookup: https://postcodes.io/

The repository contains the finished brief and documentation. Original research attachments and the internal development appraisal are excluded.
